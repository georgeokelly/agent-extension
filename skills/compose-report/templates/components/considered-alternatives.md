## ⟨N⟩. Considered Alternatives

**Intro-Summary**: ⟨1-2 sentences naming the alternatives and the criteria used to compare them⟩

> A design or feasibility report without explicit alternatives is incomplete; reviewers will assume cherry-picking. List ≥ 2 alternatives (including the chosen one) with quantitative criteria.
